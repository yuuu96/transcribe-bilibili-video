# Light Clean Verbatim

Use these rules for the readable Markdown and mobile HTML. The raw timestamp transcript remains unchanged for verification.

## Preserve

- Keep all substantive statements, judgments, analysis, examples, questions, interactions, emotional expressions, uncertainty, contradictions, and unfinished thoughts.
- Keep the original order and the timestamp of the first retained utterance in each paragraph.
- Keep natural spoken phrasing. Do not turn the transcript into an essay or reorganize the argument.
- Keep repetition when it carries emphasis, rhythm, interaction, hesitation with meaning, or emotion.

## Clean

- Remove an isolated filler sound only when it contributes no meaning. Preserve `嗯` and similar sounds when they acknowledge, answer, emphasize, interact, or express emotion.
- Remove stutters, abandoned duplicate openings, and mechanical ASR repetition.
- Repair punctuation, obvious sentence breaks, and paragraph boundaries according to the speaker's delivery.
- Prefer compact paragraphs centered on one continuous thought; start a new paragraph after a meaningful pause or topic transition.

## Correct

- Correct an ASR error only when the surrounding context or the corresponding audio makes the intended wording clear.
- Check repeated proper nouns, company names, people, technical terms, abbreviations, figures, and dates for consistency.
- For suspicious wording, listen to the matching audio interval. If it remains unclear, write `[存疑：听音]` at that position or retain the phonetic wording with the marker.
- Do not silently invent missing content, complete an unfinished thought, reconcile a contradiction, or improve the speaker's argument.

## Reject

- No summary, outline, viewpoint extraction, interpretation, evaluation, fact-check, explanatory expansion, or new conclusion.
- No merging distant passages or moving content between timestamps.
- No deletion merely because a passage is repetitive, informal, contradictory, or off-topic when it still carries meaning.

## Final Check

Compare the beginning, middle, and end of the readable edition with the raw transcript and audio. Confirm that cleanup changed presentation rather than substance, timestamps remain ordered, and Markdown and HTML match.
